The scene

You've got 20 freeform notes. They feel like noise. But look closer: "invented a 30-day policy," "made up a return address" — that's the same problem in three outfits.

The one idea

Take your open-coding notes and group the ones that are really the same problem. Each group becomes a failure mode — named, countable, fixable.

Worked example. Twenty refund-bot notes collapse into 4 failure modes. "Fabricates a policy" hits 6 times at high severity → that's your #1.
"The AI makes mistakes" is a shrug. "It fabricates policies 30% of the time" is a roadmap. ↗ Share
Tomorrow → turn your #1 failure mode into a measurement you can trust.
